    How XP, Drachma and Ambrosia work

    Pebble (easy)10 XP · 7 Dr
    Stone (medium)25 XP · 19 Dr
    Boulder (hard)50 XP · 37 Dr
    Monument (very hard)80 XP · 60 Dr
    Done100%
    Partly donethe share you did
    Gave up, or missed the day−50% XP
    Perfect day (2+ tasks, all done)+20 XP · +70 Dr
    More than 5 tasks in a daythe extra ones pay 25% Dr
    Big goal: Hill / Mountain / City / Earth150 / 400 / 900 / 2000 XP
    Win streak of 3 / 7 / 14 / 30 days×1.1 / 1.25 / 1.4 / 1.5 XP
    A week with 4+ active days+2 Ambrosia
    Each achievement+1 Ambrosia
    Streak Freeze / Pardon5 / 3 Ambrosia

    XP decides your rank and can never be bought. Drachma is earned by doing and buys most of the wardrobe. Ambrosia is rare: a few drops come from good weeks, achievements and new ranks. It buys Streak Freezes, Pardons and the Ambrosia collection. Penalties take XP, never Drachma.
